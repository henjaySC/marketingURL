Learn by doing

Every discovery has a story.

Echoes of the Past combines exploration and interactive tasks to make selected topics in Philippine history more engaging.

🗺️

Explore 3D Environments

Move through selected, historically inspired Philippine locations and use the minimap and expanded map to find objectives.

💬

Meet Historical Figures

Interact with historical characters and learn about their lives, contributions, and place in Philippine history through dialogue.

🧭

Complete Questlines

Learn through biography activities, historical site visits, and artifact-finding objectives tracked in the Quest Log.

🏺

Build an Artifact Collection

Discover bonus collectibles while exploring and view each collected item's name, associated owner, and historical information.

🎓

Test Your Knowledge

Answer randomized multiple-choice history quizzes after completing a historical figure's questline. Retry when needed.

📈

Track Learning Progress

Review completed quests and quizzes, check results, and see a completion summary of your journey through the game.

How to play

Follow the clues, complete objectives, and use what you learn to answer the quizzes.

Explore

Use the on-screen joystick, camera controls, minimap, and quest markers to navigate the environment.

Talk & Accept

Approach a historical figure with an interaction indicator, read the dialogue, and accept available quests.

Discover

Visit designated locations, interact with required objects, and collect separate bonus artifacts along the way.

Learn & Review

Complete the objectives, take the history quiz, and check your quest and quiz progress.

A supplementary learning experience

Play with curiosity. Learn from the past.

Echoes of the Past is designed to complement traditional learning materials—not replace them—by giving players an interactive way to explore selected topics in Philippine history.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is Echoes of the Past?

It is a 3D quest-based mobile educational game that introduces selected topics in Philippine history through exploration, dialogue, quests, artifact collection, and quizzes.

Who is the game for?

The project is intended for students, young learners, and others interested in Philippine history, with a target audience of ages seven and above.

Can I change the language?

Yes. The game includes English and Filipino language settings.

Does the game replace classroom learning?

No. It is intended as a supplementary learning resource alongside traditional educational materials.
